# Generative AI for Aviation Management Capstone

## ✈️ Travel Smart at the Airport — GenAI Passenger-Awareness Campaign

### 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how **Generative AI can support aviation-management communication** through the development of a passenger-awareness campaign.

The campaign, **“Travel Smart at the Airport,”** focuses on encouraging domestic airline passengers to use reliable and verified airline and airport information before travelling.

The project addresses the problem of passengers relying on incomplete or unverified travel information, particularly information shared through social media. Generative AI is used for content drafting, prompt development, visual concepts, and presentation preparation, while human review remains essential for verification and final approval.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Encourage passengers to check official airline and airport information.
* Create clear and professional passenger-awareness content.
* Demonstrate responsible use of Generative AI in aviation.
* Reduce unsupported or misleading claims.
* Protect passenger privacy.
* Maintain human review and approval throughout the workflow.

## 🏢 Business Problem

Passengers may arrive at airports without checking their latest flight information or understanding where to find official travel instructions. Unverified information from social media can create confusion.

The campaign addresses this issue by creating awareness content that encourages passengers to verify important travel information through authorised airline and airport sources.

## 👥 Target Audience

**Domestic airline passengers**

The campaign is designed to provide simple, clear and professional travel-awareness messages that can help passengers prepare before arriving at the airport.

## 📝 Prompt Engineering

The project demonstrates how simple prompts can be improved by adding:

* Role
* Context
* Task
* Audience
* Constraints
* Output format
* Tone
* Verification requirements

For example, the campaign planning prompt was refined to specify the aviation communication role, target audience, campaign name, restrictions and expected output. This helped produce more focused content while reducing unsupported operational claims.

## 🔍 Verification & Hallucination Audit

A major part of the project is checking AI-generated information before publication.

The project specifically avoids presenting unsupported information such as:

* Specific baggage allowances
* Guaranteed shorter queues
* Guaranteed flight performance
* Unverified security procedures
* Unsupported statistics
* Invented airport policies

Unsupported claims are removed or marked for verification rather than being presented as facts.

### Verification Method

```text
Claim
  ↓
Source
  ↓
Match
  ↓
Context
  ↓
Action
```

No AI-generated claim is automatically treated as factual. The supporting source must confirm the claim, while relevant conditions must remain visible.

## 🔐 Privacy & Compliance

The project follows privacy and responsible-AI principles.

* No real passenger personal information is used.
* No passport details or booking references are included.
* Confidential operational information is excluded.
* Placeholders are used where necessary.
* Public GitHub files are reviewed before publication.
* Unsupported claims are not presented as verified facts.

The repository should not contain passwords, credentials, confidential documents or unauthorised copyrighted material.

## 👨‍💼 Human Review

Human judgement remains an essential part of the workflow.

Humans are responsible for:

* Checking authoritative sources
* Verifying aviation information
* Reviewing policy claims
* Protecting personal information
* Approving passenger-facing communication
* Reviewing visual accuracy
* Making final publication decisions

Generative AI supports the work but does not replace human decision-making.

## 📚 Key Learnings

1. **Better prompts produce more useful and controlled outputs.**
2. **AI-generated information must be verified before business use.**
3. **Human judgement remains essential in aviation communication.**
4. Constraints can help reduce unsupported AI-generated claims.
5. AI-generated visuals require accuracy and ethical review.
6. Reliable sources are important when working with aviation-related information.

## ⚠️ Responsible AI Statement

Generative AI tools were used to support drafting, summarisation, visual creation and presentation development. All generated outputs were reviewed for accuracy, tone, compliance, context, privacy and source support.

The project team remains responsible for the final submitted work and decisions.
